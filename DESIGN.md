## Brand & Style

The design system embodies a synthesis of subterranean gothic fantasy and deep-trench science fiction—termed "Abyssal Futurism." The visual narrative evokes feelings of isolation, quiet dread, awe-inspiring scale, and ancient forgotten technology sleeping under kilometers of oceanic pressure and cosmic rock.

Key stylistic pillars:
- **Atmospheric Dark Glassmorphism & HUD Precision:** Translucent deep-abyss plates floating above ambient particle fields, reinforced with sharp, angular framing and telemetry markers.
- **Bioluminescent Phosphorescence:** Accent colors must feel like light emitted from abyssal organisms or dormant alien conduits—soft inner blooms combined with sharp laser edges.
- **Runic Vector Accents:** Corner cut-outs (chamfers), 45-degree angled brackets, and segmented telemetry nodes reminiscent of deciphered relics.
- **Tone & Mood:** Quiet, deliberate, high-stakes, cinematic. Interactions feature measured decays, atmospheric hums, and low-latency tactile feedback.

## Colors

The palette revolves around perpetual void-depths punctuated by high-potency photon surges.

- **Primary (`#00f5d4` - Bioluminescent Cyan):** Reserved for active systems, focus indicators, vitality pulses, critical telemetry alerts, and core interactive states.
- **Secondary (`#6366f1` - Arcane Violet):** Denotes esoteric lore, latent abilities, anomalous nodes, and depth corruption mechanics.
- **Tertiary (`#38bdf8` - Spectral Teal/Ice):** Used for tactical data overlays, secondary HUD trackers, and navigation waypoints.
- **Neutral Dark Tiers:**
  - `Void Base` (`#03070d`): Canvas backdrop, empty space.
  - `Abyss Navy` (`#050b14`): Primary viewport, container foundation.
  - `Trench Surface` (`#0a1128`): Card backplates, modal panels.
  - `Bathypelagic Elevation` (`#0f1b3d`): Elevated chips, interactive hover fields, header rails.
- **Text & Glyph Levels:**
  - `High Luminance` (`#e2f8ff`): Primary headers, crucial values.
  - `Submerged White` (`#94a3b8`): Descriptive body text, inactive HUD labels.
  - `Faded Chasm` (`#475569`): Grid telemetry lines, structural dividers, inactive slots.

## Typography

The type system balances mythic grandeur with cold diagnostic telemetry:

- **Bodoni Moda (Display & Primary Headlines):** High-contrast, razor-sharp serif providing gothic gravitas, ancient stone engravings, and grand zone titlecards. Headline letters should frequently leverage uppercase styling with slight positive tracking in smaller variants.
- **Space Grotesk (Body & Structural Navigation):** A technical geometric sans with subtle idiosyncratic quirks, providing optimal legibility for inventory lore, quest logs, and dialogue sequences without breaking the futuristic setting.
- **JetBrains Mono (Telemetry & HUD Data):** Precise, non-proportional, and clinical. Used for depth meters, weapon resonance ratings, slot coordinates, keybindings, and micro status indicators. Always uppercase for `label-sm` and `label-md`.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on a flexible 12-column tactical grid designed to accommodate widescreen HUD anchors, floating diegetic interfaces, and inventory matrices.

- **Safe Zones & Edge Anchoring:** Outer borders conform to strict margin constraints (`margin` for 1080p/4K desktop viewports, `margin-mobile` for handheld screens). Key game status trackers (health, energy, depth gauge, mini-map) pin tightly to viewport anchors leaving the viewport center open for visual immersion.
- **Density Scaling:**
  - *HUD & In-Game Overlays:* High density with minimal gap spacing (`space-xs` to `space-sm`) to maximize visible play area.
  - *Lore Compendiums & Menus:* Generous breathing room (`space-lg` to `space-2xl`) allowing dark void backgrounds to set an ominous pace.
- **Breakpoints:**
  - `Compact (Handheld / Steam Deck / Mobile)`: < 768px (4-column flow, side panels collapse to full-bleed modal drawers).
  - `Medium (Tablet / Compact Laptop)`: 768px - 1024px (8-column hybrid, split panes).
  - `Expanded (Desktop / Console Widescreen)`: > 1024px (12-column persistent HUD, multi-bracket inventory spreads).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use soft natural lighting; it relies strictly on layered deep-sea density, chromatic back-buffering, and luminescent projection:

- **Level 0 (Abyssal Void):** Pure base `#03070d` with intermittent 2% noise / scanline textures and slow radial gradients of `#0a1128`.
- **Level 1 (Docked HUD Panels & Inactive Containers):** `#050b14` with 65% opacity and a `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`. Outlined with a 1px border of `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.12)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Plates & Modals):** `#0a1128` at 80% opacity, `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`. Outer outline highlighted with `rgba(0, 245, 212, 0.25)` and a distant perimeter glow: `0 0 24px rgba(0, 245, 212, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Interactive Elements & Overlays):** `#0f1b3d` with full opacity. Borders ignite via cyan or violet highlights with sharp neon bloom: `0 0 16px rgba(0, 245, 212, 0.4), inset 0 0 8px rgba(0, 245, 212, 0.15)`.
- **Level 4 (Critical Anomalies & Focus Locks):** Chromatic edge shift utilizing secondary arcane violet glows (`0 0 30px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.5)`) to communicate magical corruption or alien interference.

## Shapes

The shape system utilizes deliberate angular precision:

- **Strict Zero Radius:** Base `roundedness` is set to `0`. No soft organic roundness is permitted; forms must feel machined, chiseled from deep bedrock, or formed by crystalline growth.
- **Chamfers & Angled Notches:** Interactive panels, buttons, and HUD brackets feature 45-degree corner clippings (typically `clip-path: polygon(...)` with cuts of 4px to 12px on opposing corners, such as top-left and bottom-right).
- **Geometric Sightlines:** Progress meters, vitals, and structural frames utilize segmented rhomboid or parallelogram shapes spaced 2px apart.

## Components

### Buttons & Action Triggers
- **Primary Action (Resonance Pulse):** Flat background in `#00f5d4` with `#050b14` bold monospace typography. Slanted chamfer cut on the top-right corner. Hover emits a bright teal diffusion bloom (`0 0 20px rgba(0, 245, 212, 0.6)`) and shifts letter tracking outward.
- **Secondary Action (Submerged Glass):** Background in `rgba(15, 27, 61, 0.6)` with a 1px border in `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.3)`. Text in `#e2f8ff`. Hover ignites the border to full `#38bdf8` with an interior gradient wash.
- **Destructive/Anomalous Action:** Deep violet base (`#6366f1`) with warning glyphs, radiating a high-frequency purple chromatic fringe.

### Cards & Relic Containers
- Constructed from multi-layered glass (`#0a1128` at 75% opacity).
- Framed by micro-notches at each corner and tiny rune-bracket watermarks (`+` crosshairs or diagonal vector ticks) in `#475569`.
- When an item slot contains a relic or weapon, the card projects an inner ambient glow corresponding to the rarity or essence tier (Cyan for Tech, Violet for Void/Arcane).

### Input Fields & Terminal Slates
- Inactive state: Flat `#050b14` field with a subtle bottom-border indicator (`1px solid #475569`).
- Focused state: Glowing cyan under-bar accompanied by vertical bracket guides flanking the left and right edges `[ | ]`. Terminal caret renders as a solid glowing block using `JetBrains Mono`.

### Chips, Tags & Status Indicators
- Microscopic, high-density elements with all-caps monospace labels (`label-sm`).
- Bordered in razor-thin 1px outlines with solid color dot indicators (e.g., `#00f5d4` for Connected/Synthesized, `#6366f1` for Corrupted/Dormant).

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes, Toggles & Radios)
- **Checkboxes:** Diamond or angled square frames. Checked state activates an illuminated cyan center core with zero border-radius.
- **Radio Nodes:** Concentric hollow diamonds that fill with an expanding spectral pulse when selected.
- **Sliders & Gauges:** Segmented tick tracks evoking submersible oxygen bars and bio-energy cells, with numeric readouts displayed in real-time.

### Specialized Gaming HUD Elements
- **Vitals Vane:** Angled dual bars (Health in Bioluminescent Cyan, Void Resonance in Arcane Violet) segmented into dynamic pip blocks.
- **Depth Altimeter:** Vertical tick-mark gauge anchored to the right screen margin displaying negative elevation in meters (`-XXXXm`), running in variable-speed monospace.
- **Dialogue Slate:** Cinematic widescreen overlay framed by hairline cyan rules, Bodoni Moda character monikers, and Space Grotesk narrative text with animated typewriter cadence.
