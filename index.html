An Empire Drowned in Its Own Ambition

Ten thousand years ago, the Kingdom of Aethelgard cast their sovereign gods into the abyssal oceanic trench, harnessing their celestial marrow to fuel eternal dynastic engines. The marrow turned black. The waters swallowed their towers in a single night.

Now, the pulse has quickened. Sickness rises from the trenches into the surface seas. As the lone Diver chosen by the slumbering deity, you must descend through four tiers of forgotten architecture, confront the warped scions of the old empire, and choose whether to rekindle the dying heart or shatter the trench forever.
